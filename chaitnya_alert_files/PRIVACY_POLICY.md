# Privacy Policy for Chaitanya Alert

**Last Updated: January 2025**

## Introduction

Chaitanya Alert ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our messaging apps notification management application with advanced features including pattern profiles, backup/sync capabilities, and subscription services.

## Information Collection and Use

### What We Collect
The app collects and stores the following information **locally on your device only**:

1. **Messaging App Notification Data**
   - Sender name (contact or group name)
   - Message content
   - Timestamp of notification
   - Source app identifier (WhatsApp, Telegram, Discord, Signal, and other supported messaging apps)

2. **Notification Preferences**
   - Contact/group names and optional display labels you configure
   - Custom sound file selections and volume settings
   - Vibration and flashlight pattern preferences
   - Regex patterns for flexible contact matching
   - Multi-app preferences for the same contact across different messaging platforms
   - Priority ordering and loop settings for notification patterns

3. **Pattern Profiles**
   - Profile names, descriptions, and visual identifiers (colors, icons)
   - Time-based scheduling settings (start/end times, days of the week)
   - Profile-specific notification overrides (sound, volume, vibration, flashlight)
   - Profile activation history and automatic switching preferences

4. **App Configuration**
   - Supported messaging app settings and custom app additions
   - Silent mode override preferences
   - Accessibility settings (high contrast, large text preferences)
   - Onboarding completion status and user preference settings

5. **Backup and Sync Data**
   - Local backup files with compression and metadata
   - Device identification for multi-device synchronization
   - Backup scheduling preferences and auto-backup settings
   - Sync conflict resolution data for handling data inconsistencies

6. **Subscription Information**
   - Premium subscription status and purchase details
   - Google Play billing tokens and order information
   - Subscription expiry and renewal data

### How We Use Information
- Display notification history within the app
- Play custom sounds, vibration, and flashlight patterns for specific contacts/groups
- Manage pattern profiles and automatic time-based switching
- Create and restore local backups of your preferences and settings
- Handle multi-device synchronization when backup/sync features are used
- Manage premium subscription features and billing
- Provide accessibility enhancements and personalized user experience
- All processing happens locally on your device

### Data Storage and Sharing
- **Local Primary**: All data is primarily stored in a local database on your device
- **Optional Cloud Backup**: When you choose to use backup/sync features, encrypted backups may be stored in cloud storage services you authorize
- **No Analytics**: We do not collect usage statistics or analytics data
- **No Third-Party Sharing**: Your notification data and preferences are never shared with third parties
- **Google Play Billing**: Subscription information is processed through Google Play's secure billing system
- **Device Sync**: When enabled, backup data may be synchronized across your own devices using authorized cloud storage

## Permissions

The app requires the following permissions:

1. **Notification Access**
   - Required to read messaging app notifications
   - Must be manually enabled in Android settings
   - Used solely to capture notifications from supported messaging apps

2. **Post Notifications** (Android 13+)
   - Required to show app notifications
   - Used for profile quick toggle notifications and app functionality

3. **Vibrate**
   - Used for custom vibration patterns in notification alerts
   - Part of the enhanced notification experience

4. **Billing** (Google Play)
   - Required for premium subscription features
   - Processed securely through Google Play Billing Library
   - Used only for in-app purchase verification and management

5. **Schedule Exact Alarm** (Android 12+)
   - Required for precise time-based profile activation
   - Used to automatically switch profiles at scheduled times
   - Ensures profiles activate at exact specified times even in Doze mode

6. **Use Exact Alarm**
   - Alternative permission for exact alarm scheduling
   - Used when Schedule Exact Alarm is not available

7. **Receive Boot Completed**
   - Required to reschedule profile alarms after device restart
   - Ensures time-based profiles continue working after reboot
   - Used only to restore scheduled profile activations

## Data Security

- **Local Security**: Primary data storage uses Android's secure app-specific storage
- **Encryption**: Backup files use compression and checksums for data integrity
- **Cloud Security**: When using backup/sync features, data is encrypted before cloud storage
- **Access Control**: Only authorized cloud storage services you configure can access backup data
- **Device Isolation**: Each device maintains its own local database with sync capabilities
- **Secure Deletion**: Data is permanently deleted when you uninstall the app
- **Billing Security**: Subscription data is handled through Google Play's secure infrastructure

## User Rights and Data Control

You have complete control over your data:
- **Notification Management**: View, delete individual notifications, or clear all notification history
- **Preference Control**: Add, edit, or remove notification preferences for any contact or group
- **Profile Management**: Create, modify, or delete pattern profiles and their schedules
- **Backup Control**: Create manual backups, configure auto-backup settings, or disable backup features entirely
- **Sync Management**: Control which devices can access your synced data and resolve sync conflicts
- **Subscription Control**: Manage premium features and cancel subscriptions through Google Play
- **Complete Removal**: Uninstall the app to permanently remove all local data
- **Export/Import**: Export your preferences to backup files and import them when needed
- **Accessibility Options**: Customize app appearance and interaction methods for your needs
